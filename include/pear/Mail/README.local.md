PEAR Mail (Local Note)
======================
Mail send/parse helpers from PEAR.

Contents
--------
- mail.php, sendmail.php, smtp.php, smtpmx.php – transport helpers.
- mime.php, mimeDecode.php, mimePart.php – MIME encoding/decoding utilities.
- RFC822.php – address parsing.
- mock.php, null.php – test/no-op transports.

Notes
-----
- Vendor code; do not edit. Mail handling in osTicket relies on these helpers.
